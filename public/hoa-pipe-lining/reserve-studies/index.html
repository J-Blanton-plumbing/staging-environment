Our Process

What You Get, Step by Step

Step 01

Site Walk & Scheduling

We coordinate with your property manager on building access, previous history of back-ups and other building specific nuances. 

Step 02

Camera and Line Tracing - Inspection Day

Our crews run a sewer camera through every accessible common area line, building by building, and record footage of the full run.

Step 03

Report Generating 

Every foot of the pipe is carefully analyzed and  reviewed by HOA Diagnostic Manager for accuracy and repair suggestions

Step 04

Report Delivery

You receive a pipe-by-pipe condition table, a prioritized repair list as well as general educational information.

Step 05

Planning Walkthrough

An HOA advisor performs virtual presenation and walks your board through the findings, answers questions, and helps prioritize repairs against your reserve fund. 

Pricing & Timeline

Things to Consider

Pricing is determined by  number of buildings and linear footage inspected. Most communities receive a completed report within 2–4 weeks of the on-site inspection. Call your HOA advisor for a quote sized to your association.

How is this different from a general reserve study?

A general reserve study estimates remaining life from age and industry averages. Ours is built from camera-verified findings inside your actual pipes.

Do you need access to units or basements?

Most common area lines are accessible from cleanouts and main runs. We'll flag in advance if any unit access is needed.

How often should we repeat the study?

We typically recommend revisiting findings every 3–5 years, or sooner after any major repair or lining work.

What happens after we get the report?

Your advisor walks the board through prioritized findings and can quote lining or repair work for anything flagged urgent.
