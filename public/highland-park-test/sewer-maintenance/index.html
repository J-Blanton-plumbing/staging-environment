Prevention Pays Off

Why Does Sewer Line Maintenance Matter?

Sewer line maintenance catches problems while they're still small and prevents them from becoming expensive fixes down the line. Here's what regular checkups can do for you:

Catch Early Root Intrusion

Roots are caught before they turn into a blockage.

Flag Aging Pipe

An aging or clay pipe gets flagged before it cracks.

Stop Small Slow Drains

A minor slow drain doesn't get the chance to turn into a full backup.

Keep Small Fixes Small

A small fix doesn't turn into a big one.

Maintenance Guidelines

How Often Does a Highland Park Sewer Line Need Maintenance?

How often your line needs a checkup depends mostly on your pipe's age and what's growing near it. Nearly half of Highland Park homes were built before 1960, and many sit on lots with mature trees, so most homes here land in the first two rows.

Risk TierRecommended FrequencyWhy
Older homes (clay, cast iron, or other aging pipe)At least once a yearThese materials are more prone to cracking, shifting, and joint failure, especially as they age
Homes with mature trees nearbyEvery 1 to 2 yearsTree roots are the leading cause of sewer line blockages and work into small cracks over time
Newer homes (PVC or modern plastic pipe)Every 3 to 5 yearsThese materials resist roots and corrosion better, so less frequent checks are usually enough

Not sure which category fits your home? A first inspection tells us exactly where you stand.

Local Knowledge

Sewer Maintenance for Highland Park Homes

Two facts make regular checkups worth it here: the line is yours, and the trees are big.

You Own the Line to the Main

In Highland Park, the homeowner owns and maintains the private sewer line from the house to the city main. The city's flood tips recommend having it checked for roots and debris. A maintenance visit is that check.

Ravines, Trees and Clay Joints

Highland Park's ravines and tree-lined streets put a lot of roots near older clay pipe. Catching early growth on camera costs far less than a backup in a finished basement.

Sewer Maintenance Questions From Highland Park Homeowners

Older clay or cast iron lines: once a year. Mature trees nearby: every one to two years.

It depends on your pipe's age and what's around it. Older pipes, like clay or cast iron, benefit from a check at least once a year. Homes with mature trees nearby should aim for every one to two years. With nearly half of Highland Park homes built before 1960, most lines here fall into one of those two groups.

A camera inspection, an honest read on your line and a light interior clean.

A maintenance visit starts with a camera inspection to check your line's current condition. From there, we give you an honest read on what we see, along with a light interior clean as part of the visit. If something bigger turns up, we'll walk you through it before recommending any next steps.

Most sewer emergencies give early warning signs. Maintenance catches them while the fix is small.

Sewer line emergencies are some of the most expensive plumbing problems a homeowner can face, and most of them don't happen without warning. Regular maintenance catches early signs, like root growth or a developing crack, while they're still a small, inexpensive fix.

Maintenance is preventive. Repair is for an actual crack, break or backup.

Maintenance is scheduled and preventive: a camera inspection and light upkeep to catch problems early. Repair is what happens once there's an actual issue, like a crack, a break, or a line that's already backing up. If your maintenance visit turns up something more serious, that's when a repair conversation starts.
