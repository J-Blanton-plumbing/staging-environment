The Basics

What Is Hydro Jetting?

Hydro jetting clears a sewer line with a high-pressure water stream, fed through the same cleanout access point used for rodding. A specialized nozzle sprays water in every direction as it moves through the pipe, scouring grease, scale, and roots off the entire pipe wall instead of just cutting a path through the middle. It's the fix for buildup that keeps coming back after rodding, or for roots that have worked into the pipe joints. Rodding opens the line back up. Hydro jetting cleans it.

Know the Signs

When Your Plumbing Needs Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting clears buildup off the entire pipe wall, not just the one clog blocking it right now. Here's how to know it's time to call:

Drains Gurgle When the Washer Empties

If your drains gurgle or the basement floor drain backs up when the washing machine empties, water can't get past something in the main line. On tree-lined streets, that something is often roots.

Roots Have Worked Into the Pipe Joints

Roots find any gap in a pipe joint and grow into the line chasing moisture. A rod clears what's blocking flow right now, but it doesn't stop what's growing at the next joint down the line.

Buildup Has Hardened on the Pipe Wall

Grease, scale, and sludge coat the inside of the pipe over time. A rod cuts a path through the middle, but it doesn't remove what's stuck to the walls.

The Clog Keeps Coming Back After Rodding

Rodding breaks through the immediate blockage, but it leaves buildup on the pipe walls that the next clog catches on. Hydro jetting clears what rodding leaves behind.

Local Knowledge

Why Highland Park Sewer Lines Clog

Highland Park has two things that are hard on sewer lines: older homes and big trees. Nearly half of the homes here were built before 1960, and about 1 in 4 before 1940. Many still have their original clay sewer pipe.

Older Clay Pipe With Aging Joints

Clay sewer pipe comes in short sections joined end to end. As a line ages and the ground shifts, those joints open small gaps. Grease and scale catch on the edges, and roots find their way in. Hydro jetting cleans the whole pipe wall, so the next clog has less to catch on.

Ravines, Mature Trees and Root Intrusion

Highland Park's ravines and tree-lined streets put a lot of roots close to sewer lines. Roots grow toward moisture and slip into older pipe at the joints. Hydro jetting cuts them out and flushes them away. Roots do grow back, so a camera inspection shows where they're getting in, and you can decide if a sewer repair makes sense.

Your Sewer Line Is Your Responsibility

In Highland Park, the homeowner owns and maintains the private sewer line from the house to the city main. When roots or buildup block it, the fix is on you, not the city. The city's own flood tips recommend having your sewer line checked for roots and debris. A clean line, backed by an overhead sewer system or other basement flood protection, gives heavy rain less of a chance to push water back into your basement.
