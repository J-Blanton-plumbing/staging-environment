What to Do During a Sewage Backup

A sewage backup is a health risk as much as a plumbing problem. Here's what to do while we're on the way:

1. Stop Using Water

Don't flush, run the dishwasher or do laundry. Every gallon that goes down a drain has nowhere to go but back up.

2. Keep People and Pets Away

Sewage carries bacteria. Keep everyone out of the affected area and don't try to clean it with bare hands.

3. Call Us 24/7

Our phone line is open around the clock, including weekends and holidays. Tell us which drains are backing up and we'll take it from there.

What Causes It

Why Sewage Backs Up Into a Home

Most backups come down to one of a few causes. The camera tells us which one you have:

Roots in the Main Line

Roots grow into older pipe at the joints and build a net that catches everything else. On tree-lined Highland Park lots, this is the most common cause we'd check first.

Grease and Buildup

Grease, wipes and scale narrow the pipe over time until one day nothing gets through.

A Broken or Sagging Pipe

A cracked clay joint or a belly in the line lets waste collect until it backs up. That calls for a sewer repair, not just a cleaning.

A Failed Ejector Pump

If your basement bathroom or laundry drains through an ejector pump, a failed pump can back up sewage even when the main line is clear.

Local Knowledge

Sewage Backups in Highland Park Homes

The median home in Highland Park was built in 1963, and nearly half were built before 1960. Older clay lines and mature trees are the combination behind most backups here.

The Private Line Is Yours

In Highland Park, the homeowner owns and maintains the private sewer line from the house to the city main. A backup that starts in that line is yours to fix, so an accurate diagnosis saves money.

Stopping the Next One

Once the line is clear, the fix that lasts depends on the cause: hydro jetting for roots and buildup, a repair for broken pipe, or an overhead sewer system for a connection that can't keep up in heavy rain. The city's flood tips also recommend backflow preventers.

Plumber First, Cleanup Second

Call a licensed plumber before a cleanup crew. If sewage is still coming in, the source has to be stopped before any cleanup is effective. We stop it, then restoration can begin.
