The Basics

What Is Hydro Jetting?

Hydro jetting clears a sewer line with a high-pressure water stream, fed through the same cleanout access point used for rodding. A specialized nozzle sprays water in every direction as it moves through the pipe, scouring grease, scale, and roots off the entire pipe wall instead of just cutting a path through the middle. It's the fix for buildup that keeps coming back after rodding, or for roots that have worked into the pipe joints. Rodding opens the line back up. Hydro jetting cleans it.

Know the Signs

When You Need Hydro Jetting

Hydro jetting clears buildup off the entire pipe wall, not just the one clog blocking it right now. Here's how to know it's time to call:

Buildup Has Hardened on the Pipe Wall

Grease, scale, and sludge coat the inside of the pipe over time. A rod cuts a path through the middle, but it doesn't remove what's stuck to the walls.

Roots Have Worked Into the Pipe Joints

Roots find any gap in a pipe joint and grow into the line chasing moisture. A rod clears what's blocking flow right now, but it doesn't stop what's growing at the next joint down the line.

The Clog Keeps Coming Back After Rodding

Rodding breaks through the immediate blockage, but it leaves buildup on the pipe walls that the next clog catches on. Hydro jetting clears what rodding leaves behind.
