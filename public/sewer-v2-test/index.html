Understanding the Options

Sewer Services We Provide

We handle everything from small sewer repairs to full line replacements. That includes camera inspections to pinpoint the problem and trenchless repair options that skip digging up your yard. Here's a look at what we offer.

Sewer Line Installation

New sewer line installation for new construction, conversions, and properties that need a first-time connection or a full system upgrade.

Learn More →

Sewer Rodding

Mechanical clearing for blockages like tree roots, grease, and debris, so water flows again fast.

Learn More →

Sewer Line Repair

Fixes for damaged sections of pipe (cracks, joint separation, root intrusion through a seam) without replacing the whole line.

Learn More →

Sewer Line Replacement

For lines too far gone for a targeted repair: extensive cracking, collapse, or pipe material breaking down along most of its length.

Learn More →

Sewer Line Maintenance

Routine service that catches small issues, like early root growth or a slow drain, before years of buildup turn into a backup in your basement.

Learn More →

Sewer Camera Inspection

A camera run through your line before we recommend anything, so you see exactly what's wrong and we walk you through the options.

Learn More →

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water clears grease, scale, and root buildup that rodding alone can't fully remove.

Learn More →

Trenchless Sewer Repair

Repairs or replaces a damaged line by lining or bursting the pipe in place, with no digging up your yard.

Learn More →

Overhead Sewer Systems

Converts a gravity-fed line into a pump-driven system that keeps sewage moving up and out, even during heavy rain.

Frequently Asked Questions

If rodding clears the drain and it stays clear, it’s usually just a clog, not pipe damage.

If a drain is slow or backing up but clears with rodding and stays clear, it’s usually a clog, not damage to the pipe. If the same drain keeps backing up after clearing, or you’re seeing multiple symptoms at once, that’s a sign the issue is bigger than a clog. We run a camera through the line to confirm exactly what’s going on before recommending a fix.

We run a small camera through your line and record everything it finds, so you see exactly what’s going on.

We run a small camera through your sewer line and record everything it finds: cracks, root intrusion, blockages, misalignment. You see the same footage we do, so you know exactly what’s wrong before we talk about how to fix it.

It depends on what’s wrong and how much work it takes. We’ll always give you a flat rate before starting.

Cost depends on what’s actually wrong: the depth of the line, how much of it needs work, and whether we can fix it without digging. We walk you through your options and give you a flat rate before any work starts. Give us a call and we’ll tell you what it’ll take for your situation.

Rodding is usually same-day. Repairs and relining take longer since they involve fixing the pipe itself.

It depends on the method. Rodding usually clears a blockage the same day. Repair or trenchless relining takes longer, since it involves fixing or replacing damaged pipe rather than just clearing it. We’ll give you a timeline specific to your line once we’ve seen what’s going on.

Yes, a sewer backup doesn’t wait for business hours, so neither do we.

Yes, a sewer backup doesn’t wait for business hours, so neither do we. If you’re seeing standing water, sewage smell, or backups into your home, call us and we’ll get someone out to you.
