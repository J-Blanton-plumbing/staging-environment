Prevention Pays Off

Why Does Sewer Line Maintenance Matter?

Sewer line maintenance catches problems while they're still small and prevents them from becoming expensive fixes down the line. Here's what regular checkups can do for you:

  • Catch early root intrusion before it turns into a blockage.
  • Flag an aging or clay pipe before it cracks.
  • Stop a minor slow drain from turning into a full backup.
  • Keep a small fix from turning into a big one.

Sewer line emergencies are among the most expensive plumbing problems a homeowner can face. Routine maintenance is the most cost-effective way to avoid them.

Maintenance Guidelines

How Often Does a Sewer Line Need Maintenance?

How often your line needs a checkup depends mostly on your pipe's age and what's growing near it. Plumbers and home inspectors generally recommend:

Risk TierRecommended FrequencyWhy
Older homes (clay, cast iron, or other aging pipe materials)At least once a yearThese materials are more prone to cracking, shifting, and joint failure, especially as they age
Homes with mature trees nearbyEvery 1 to 2 yearsTree roots are the leading cause of sewer line blockages and work into small cracks over time
Newer homes (PVC or modern plastic pipe)Every 3 to 5 yearsThese materials resist roots and corrosion better, so less frequent checks are usually enough

Not sure which category fits your home? A first inspection tells us exactly where you stand.
